About us
Our vision
Gli Amici di Boyle is a group of physicians, engineers and researchers sharing a clear vision: high-quality research, education and communication in extreme environment medicine. The focus is hyperbaric and diving medicine, but the scope extends further: space medicine, mountain medicine, military medicine and critical patient management in high-complexity scenarios.

In progress
What we are building
The Boyle Library — an open archive of articles, books, protocols and resources on extreme environment medicine. Every member will be able to contribute by reporting resources, sharing materials and bringing their own perspective.
The Podcast section is growing rapidly: new episodes, new topics and interviews with people who truly live “under pressure” — in the ward, underwater, at altitude and in space. A dedicated YouTube channel and our social channels are ready to launch.
We are also building a section dedicated to Extreme Environment Engineering. Physicians and engineers working in the same extreme environments have far more in common than most people realise — yet they rarely speak to each other. It is time to change that.
Collaborations with several Italian universities that believe in the culture and communication of extreme environment medicine are already underway.
Ideas? Write to info@amicidiboyle.it — we are here.
