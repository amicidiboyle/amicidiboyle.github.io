About us
Our vision

Gli Amici di Boyle is a group of physicians, engineers and researchers sharing a clear vision: high-quality research, education and communication in extreme environment medicine. The focus is hyperbaric and diving medicine, but the scope extends further: space medicine, mountain medicine, military medicine and critical patient management in high-complexity scenarios.

The Network — why it's different
A network of frontline professionals

The Boyle Network is a reserved space where selected professionals meet, connect and collaborate. Each member has been chosen for their expertise, activity and operational impact in their field.

Joining the network means:

  • ✓Accesso diretto a tutti i membri — email personali, profili estesi, aree di competenza
  • ✓Coinvolgimento su congressi, editoriali, workshop prima che siano aperti al pubblico
  • ✓Newsletter riservata con contenuti che circolano solo all'interno della community
  • ✓Card pubblica sul portale — il tuo biglietto da visita professionale, sempre aggiornato
  • ✓Access to the Boyle Library (work in progress)

In progress
What we're building

The Boyle Library — an open archive of articles, books, protocols and resources on extreme environment medicine. Every network member will be able to contribute: flagging resources, sharing materials, bringing their perspective.

The Podcast section is growing rapidly: new episodes, new topics and interviews with those who truly live “under pressure” — in the ward, underwater, at altitude, in space. A dedicated YouTube channel and social media channels are ready to launch.

We are building a section dedicated to Extreme Environment Engineering. Physicians and engineers working in the same extreme environments have far more in common than one might think — yet they rarely talk to each other. It is time to change that.

Collaborations with several Italian universities that believe in the culture and communication of extreme environment medicine are already underway.

If you have ideas or want to contribute, write to info@amicidiboyle.it — we are here.
