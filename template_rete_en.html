About us
Our vision
Gli Amici di Boyle is a group of physicians, engineers and researchers sharing a clear vision: high-quality research, education and communication in extreme environment medicine. The focus is hyperbaric and diving medicine, but the scope extends further: space medicine, mountain medicine, military medicine and critical patient management in high-complexity scenarios.
The Network
A network of frontline professionals
The Boyle Network is a private space where selected professionals meet, connect and collaborate. Each member has been chosen for their expertise, activity and operational impact in their field.
Joining the network means:
✓
 Direct access to all members — personal email addresses, extended profiles and areas of expertise
✓
 Involvement in conferences, editorials and workshops before they are open to the public
✓
 A private newsletter with content circulating only within the community
✓
 A public card on the portal — your professional calling card, always up to date
✓
 Access to the Boyle Library (work in progress)
Your profile
Personalise it — and make yourself available
After entering your code, the edit icon ✏️ will appear on your card. From there you can personalise your bio, add links to ResearchGate, ORCID, Google Scholar, your personal website and publications.
You can also indicate your availability: research collaborations, speaking at courses or conferences, or being featured on Luigi Tarsia’s Sotto Pressione podcast — 27+ episodes on extreme environment medicine.
Courses and events
Your visibility on the portal
Network members can publish their events directly on amicidiboyle.it/rete. If an initiative is high-quality and aligned with our vision, we will give it visibility across the community and feature it in our newsletter.
In progress
What we are building
The Boyle Library — an open archive of articles, books, protocols and resources on extreme environment medicine. Every network member will be able to contribute by reporting resources, sharing materials and bringing their own perspective.
The Podcast section is growing rapidly: new episodes, new topics and interviews with people who truly live “under pressure” — in the ward, underwater, at altitude and in space. A dedicated YouTube channel and our social channels are ready to launch.
We are also building a section dedicated to Extreme Environment Engineering. Physicians and engineers working in the same extreme environments have far more in common than most people realise — yet they rarely speak to each other. It is time to change that.
Collaborations with several Italian universities that believe in the culture and communication of extreme environment medicine are already underway.
If you have ideas or want to contribute something, write to info@amicidiboyle.it — we are here.
